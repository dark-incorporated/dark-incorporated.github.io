# Everything gooey has, by what you want to do

The README names a handful of things; this page is the whole list, one section per job, with the names a host uses and a link to the page that goes into it. [What a host touches](host-api.md) is the same list by kind (resources, components, systems) and [the widgets](widgets.md) is every widget in one table. The first seven sections are widgets and screens; the rest are the layout parts for a tool's own panels, which arrived with G-82 to G-90 and are newest.

## Skin the whole interface from one value

`GooeySkin` is plain data with four presets (dark, parchment, a high-contrast one held to 7:1 and a compact `Tool` one), a `problems()` check against the WCAG 2.2 contrast and target-size rules, `density: Density::Compact` for a desktop tool's rows (half the padding, a 24 px least target), design tokens (`spacing`, `radii`, `type_scale` and a `surfaces` ladder of five fills whose every step `problems()` holds to at least ΔL\* 2, drawn by `surface(n)`; your own named colours go in `tones`, drawn by `Role::Tone` and checked pair by pair with `tone_problems`; `hover`, `pressed`, `held`, `card` and friends are the states your own controls draw by `Role::Pressed` and the like), and a `SkinScope(skin)` on any node restyles just that subtree (a preview of a theme, an editor's own chrome next to a game's skin): `GooeySkin::from_seed(PaletteSeed { background, text, accent })` derives a whole checked skin from three colours, `GooeySkin::desk()` is a desktop tool's neutral look built that way, and `elevation` holds its shadows as CSS-style data: [skins and checks](skins.md).

`color_field("Tint", color)` is a swatch and a hex field (`#ff8800`) that open a picker, `FieldProblem::error("A name is needed")` on a row says what is wrong under it, `icon(source)` is your art sized and tinted by the skin, `progress(0.4)` and `spinner()` are honest waiting, `radial(["Pistol", "Rifle", ..], 0)` is a weapon wheel a stick, the pointer or the D-pad points at and South chooses ## Scroll a list, show a table or a tree, split the screen and dock panels

`list(4.5, children![..])` cuts the fifth row so the player sees there is more; `table(columns, rows)` is ten thousand sortable rows and `tree(nodes)` ten thousand nodes that open and close, each in a dozen entities (typing a name goes to it, in a list and an open menu too; `Tree::relabel` and `Tree::insert` change one node without reading the rest); `split(SplitAxis::Horizontal, a, b)` is two panes and a divider a drag or the keys move (collapse a pane, with `CollapseSplit` from code too, and it peeks over the content while the pointer rests at its edge); `Dock(DockLayout::group(["reload"]))` on a node of your own is a dock anywhere — a bottom strip, a sidebar — and it grows the body its registered panels are built in, leaving your `Node` and your `DropTarget` alone; `DragPayload(piece)` on a row and `DropTarget::<Piece>::new()` on a panel is drag and drop (one `Dropped<Piece>` a drop, a ghost, a mark that says no before the pointer is let go; Alt+M and the arrows or a pad do it with no pointer); `card_grid([Card::new("Harbour").image(thumb).line("13 pieces")])` is a wrapping grid of picture cards, one tab stop, a chosen card lit; `slot_grid(5, items)` is an inventory of slots (`SlotItems` is the data, the grid is one tab stop the arrows walk, Enter chooses a slot, a drag, Alt+M or a pad moves an item between slots or grids as one `SlotMoved`, `SlotRules` makes an equipment slot say no in red before the player lets go, and a lifted item frames every slot that takes it and dims the ones that do not); `dock(DockLayout)` is tab groups of your registered panels the player rearranges by dragging a tab; a tool's chrome is `toolbar(..)` of `icon_button`s and a `toggle_group` (the last items go behind a More button when the bar is too narrow), `status_bar(left, right)` (the lowest priority hides first) and `select(..)`, a choice that opens a filtering list past eight options, and `section(title, open, children)` folds a group of widgets under a header, its state kept by `SectionId`, and (feature `reflect`) `inspector(InspectTarget::component::<T>(entity))` builds the rows for a reflected component or resource, one widget per field: [lists](lists.md), [tables](tables.md), [trees](trees.md), [split panes](splits.md), [docks](docks.md), [toolbars](toolbars.md), [status bar](status-bar.md), [select](select.md), [inventory](inventory.md), [drag and drop](drag-drop.md).

## Reach every screen with keys or a pad, and back out of it

arrows and the D-pad move focus to the nearest widget, an open dialog holds it (`confirm("Leave?", "Progress is lost.", ["Leave", "Stay"])` is a whole one: the safe choice focused, Escape answers it, one `ConfirmChosen`; `hold_button("Hold to delete", 1.2)` confirms only when held, and the player's `HoldSettings` shorten the hold or make it a tap; `primary_button("New game")` is the screen's one main action, filled with the accent and lifted under the pointer), the ring appears the moment a key is pressed, Escape or the pad's B is one `Cancel` event that reaches the dialog or screen you are leaving, `FocusRegion`s split a tool's screen into parts F6 walks between (a `viewport()` keeps the arrow keys for the host), and `prompts()` says so in the corner, in the words of the device the player used last ("Esc Back", then "B Back" the frame they pick up a pad); a screen reader is told every widget's role, name and state from the words on screen, and can press, focus, step and set them (the same events the keyboard sends), nothing to write: [navigation](navigation.md), [focus regions](focus-regions.md), [prompts](prompts.md), [the dialog](widgets.md#a-dialog), [hold buttons](hold-buttons.md), [accessibility](accessibility.md).

## Say something, or open a menu, without building a layout

`commands.spawn(toast("Game saved"))` stacks, times itself to its own length and ages on real time (`.action("Undo", id)` adds a button, `.sticky()` keeps an error until dismissed, repeats merge into "x3", `notification_center()` lists the log, a row with its entry's id, a count and buttons that send `NotificationAction`, and `.unlogged()` / `.logged_as(id)` keep a toast and its journal line one entry), and `popover(anchor, Placement::Below)` opens a panel beside any widget or at the pointer, kept inside the window and closed by Escape or a press elsewhere: [toasts](toasts.md), [popovers](popovers.md); and `menu_bar(children![menu_title("File", children![menu_item("Open...").with_shortcut(..), separator(), ..])])` is a desktop menu bar for a tool, F10 and the arrows to walk it, with checks, radio runs, submenus and disabled items that say why: [menus](menus.md); every dialog and menu eases into place and thins out as it goes rather than vanishing, a tab that layout moves travels to its new place (`MovesSmoothly` on any node), a held card or slot gives under the press (`PressScale`), a changed HUD number swells and settles (`Pop`), panels, buttons, meters, tracks, tabs, text fields, popups and a `card_grid`'s cards can be drawn from nine-slice art the skin tints (`GooeyFrames`), an `led("Agent", LedState::Live)` row glows and says its state in a shape and a word, a section grows open and a split's collapse slides, colours ease on springs, buttons say what they do (`ButtonVariant`) (and with `motion_row`'s Reduced they fade in place and nothing travels), a health meter leaves what it lost behind its fill, and `MotionSettings::off()` turns it all off: [motion](motion.md), [presence](presence.md); and `ContextMenu(menu)` on any entity, a sprite in the world or a row of a list, opens a `context_menu(..)` at a right-click or below the focus on Shift+F10: [context menus](context-menus.md); and `commands.spawn(command_palette())` is one searchable list of every command in a `CommandList`, each with its chord and why it is off, "opn prj" finds "File: Open Project", Enter sends one `RunCommand`: [the command palette](command-palette.md).

## Keep the interface's share of the screen at every resolution, and let the pointer through a HUD

the skin's sizes scale with the window, every border and every text size stays a whole number of device pixels, and the empty part of a `screen` is `Pickable::IGNORE`; two quick clicks in one place are one `DoublePress` (a third starts a new pair, a held or dragged press pairs with nothing) and `ClickCount` tells a drag which press of a run it is: [screen size](scale.md), [over a game world](pointer.md); and `(meter(0.8, 1.0), WorldAnchor::above(enemy, 44.0))` is a health bar that rides a thing in the world (`.pinned()` keeps a beacon on the screen's edge toward it), gone with the thing: [world anchors](world-anchor.md). `Cooldown::new(8.0)` on any widget dims it and sweeps it clear clockwise with the seconds left on a chip, sends `CooldownReady` when it ends and puts the seconds in its accessible name: [cooldowns](cooldowns.md).

## Read a page of Markdown, rendered, with its pictures

`markdown_view(source, MarkdownBase::new("guide", |path| Some(assets.load(path.to_owned()))))` (feature `markdown`) draws CommonMark with tables, strikethrough and task lists in the skin's type scale in a viewport that scrolls: headings with stable ids (`MarkdownOutline`, `scroll_to_heading(view, "palette")`), links that are focusable nodes sending `MarkdownLink { dest, kind }` (the view opens nothing), pictures through `image_box` from your resolver, and a box naming its alt text and path for one that is missing, fails to load or climbs out of the base with `..` (`MarkdownImageRefused`); the page is built when its source changes, never per frame, and `markdown::images(source)`, `links` and `outline` list what the view draws from the same parse ([markdown](markdown.md)).

## Lay a tool's panel out without a `Node` literal

`row(1.0)`, `column(1.0)`, `cell(7.0)`, `image_box(plot, size)` and `overlay()` build a bench's slot rows, its label columns and its plot with the hover readout stacked on it, their gaps and widths taken from the skin so an interface size or a theme moves all of it ([layout](layout.md)).

## Say how a row lays out, without patching its node

`row_with(1.0, RowLayout { align: Some(AlignItems::Center), wrap: Some(FlexWrap::Wrap), shrink: Some(0.0), ..default() })` (or `RowLayout` as a component beside `row(1.0)`, which works inside a `Children::spawn(SpawnWith(..))` where a patched `Node` cannot), `column_with(gap, layout)` and `button_row(0.5)`, a row of buttons that stays on one centred line and wraps a gap below when the room runs out, and `fill_column(0.5)`, a column that takes the room its row leaves and can narrow to zero so the text in it wraps inside it, and `fill_region()`, the height-axis twin that holds a tree or a list and can shrink to nothing, and `sized_panel(CardWidth::Px(520.0))`, a dialog's card or a form with a width of its own in px or text sizes, never wider than its host, `placed_at(30.0, 40.0)` and `cover()`, a node at a pixel place of its host or filling it that takes no room in the flow, `status_mark()` for a job row's own `StatusKind` dot and `plain_button("Cancel")`, a button's look that stays off the `Activate` bus, and `aspect_image(splash, 480.0, 16.0 / 9.0)`, a picture that is 480 px wide until its host is narrower and keeps its ratio ([layout](layout.md))

## Choose a row of a list without moving it

`list_row()` (and `quiet_list_row()`, off the `Activate` bus) is a full-width row with its left rail always reserved: Bevy's `Selected` lights the rail in the accent and raises the fill, and no word moves ([layout](layout.md))

## A scrolling column you put your own markers and children on

`scroll_viewport(0.5, Some(Space::Px(12.0)))` is one bundle that IS the scrolling entity (read `ScrollPosition` on it, spawn your rows as its children), with gooey's rail as a sibling it places itself, keyboard focus followed into view and PageUp, PageDown, Home and End that leave a text field or table inside its own keys ([layout](layout.md))

## Let a host keep its own `display`, fill with an auto split, scroll a region

`ShownFollow` keeps writing `Node::display` yours and hides the node from focus when it is `None`; `Sizing { width: Some(Extent::Auto) }` with `TextFlow { grow: Some(1.0) }` is a split that fills what its sibling leaves; `Extent::LinesCeil(2.0)` is two lines rounded up to a device pixel; `scroll_region(0.5, Some(Space::Px(12.0)), children![..])` is a scrolling column that fills its parent with a rail lane you choose ([layout](layout.md))

## Build a frame from parts that fit

`scrim()` is a full-size layer that blocks the pointer (a modal layer; `cover()` lets it through), `GapPx(8.0)` is a gap in logical px, `Extent::Vh(100.0)` a window-sized root, `Extent::Lines(2.0)` a height that follows the text size, a `Panel`, `Surface` or `Card` takes a `RowLayout` and a `LayoutGap` like a row does, and `InkFollow` leaves a node's colours to a system of your own ([layout](layout.md))

## Paint a node's colours yourself, by state

`InkFollow` on a node makes the skin write neither its fill nor its edge (and still every other field: size, border width, radius, padding, text colour), so a colour system of your own can follow held, selected, hovered and failing; take it off and the skin paints it again ([layout](layout.md))

## Colour one node yourself

`(button("rust"), InkOverride { fill: Some(my_fill), edge: Some(my_edge), ..default() })` keeps gooey's layout and states and draws this node in your colours (a field left `None` keeps the role's, a skin change does not overwrite it, the focus ring still wins) ([layout](layout.md))

## A button that holds your own children

`button_box()` is a focusable, hover- and press-painted `Button` with a button's frame that takes any children (a picture, two texts, a badge) and puts `Role::ButtonText` on none of them ([layout](layout.md))

## Give a text its own flex

`label_with("Reviewer", TextFlow { shrink: Some(0.0), ..default() })` keeps its words' width beside a `fill_text` that gives way, and `TextFlow { grow: Some(1.0), min_width: Some(Extent::Px(0.0)), .. }` is a title that grows and may narrow to nothing ([layout](layout.md))

## Size and shape any node from the skin

`(placed_at(30.0, 40.0), Sizing { width: Some(Extent::Px(120.0)), height: Some(Extent::Px(60.0)), ..default() }, Shape { border: Sides::all(1.0), ink: Some(Ink::Accent), radius: Some(Radius::Step(1)), fill: Some(1) })` is a chip with a size, a border in the skin's line weight, rounded corners and a raised fill; `sized(Sizing { .. })` is a plain box of a width and a height, and a field left `None` leaves the node alone ([layout](layout.md))

## Push a row apart, gap a page, hide it

`row(1.0)` with `children![button("Back"), spacer(), button("Next")]` puts one at each end and two spacers centre what is between them; `fill_region_with(1.0)` is a growing region with a group gap, and `Shown(false)` on it hides it through the skin (no room, no drawing, no tab stops, no focus trap), `Shown(true)` gives back what it had, with no `Node` patched ([layout](layout.md))

## Give any node room in the skin's own steps

`(label("Section"), Spacing { margin: Sides { bottom: Some(Space::Step(2)), ..default() }, padding: Sides::x(Space::Step(1)) })` puts a margin and a padding on a text, a row, a panel or a placed node from `GooeySkin::spacing`, so a theme moves all of it, and a side left `None` leaves the node alone ([layout](layout.md))

## Build a tool's lists and strips from parts that know their size

`glyph_button("v", "Fold")` is a caret whose hit area is the skin's target however small the mark, `edge_tab("Meshes")` is a tab of a strip you select with Bevy's `Selected` whose bottom edge lights and moves nothing, `band()` is a full-width header row that is a button and shades like a list row, and `fill_text(name)` beside `count_text("145")` is a name that wraps in its own column next to a tally that never does; a label over a 3D view pane is a `WorldAnchor` with `.camera(stage)` ([layout](layout.md), `cargo run --example kit`).
